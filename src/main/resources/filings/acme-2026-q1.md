# Acme Analytics Inc. (fictional) - Quarterly Report, Q1 FY2026

Sample data for the agent-harness demo. All figures are invented.

Total revenue for Q1 FY2026 was $4,410 million, up 9% year over year. Subscription revenue was $3,530 million and professional services revenue was $880 million.

Operating income for Q1 FY2026 was $662 million, an operating margin of 15.0%. Research and development expense was $905 million.

Net income for Q1 FY2026 was $498 million, or $1.21 per diluted share.

Free cash flow for Q1 FY2026 was $710 million. The company repurchased $250 million of common stock during the quarter.

Risk factors: the company depends on three hyperscale cloud providers for hosting; a prolonged outage or price increase at any of them could materially affect gross margin.

Guidance: for Q2 FY2026 management expects revenue between $4,950 million and $5,050 million.
